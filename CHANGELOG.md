# Fletcher

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Talk to a vendor that sells ammo and Fletcher puts a full stack of the best arrows or bullets you can use there in every empty slot of your quiver or ammo pouch, then says in chat what it bought and how many slots are in use. Ammo you already carry in your bags counts, so it is not bought again.
- When the vendor also sells a better arrow or bullet that unlocks at your next level, Fletcher says so once per visit and in its tooltip, then refills anyway: an empty quiver is worse than a stack that is second best for one level.
- The bag decides the kind: a quiver gets arrows, an ammo pouch gets bullets. Other ammo already in the bag is never moved, sold or replaced.
- When the ammo you have loaded beats anything the vendor sells, nothing is bought, or only the empty slots are filled if you choose so.
- Keep gold and Spending cap limit what a refill may spend. Hold Shift while you talk to the vendor to skip a refill once, or turn Auto buy off and click the Fletcher button instead.
- The best ammo is marked with a gold border in the vendor window, across pages, and the other ammo you can use can be marked in green.
- A settings page under Options > AddOns, a right-click menu on the Fletcher button, and `/ammo` slash commands for every option.
